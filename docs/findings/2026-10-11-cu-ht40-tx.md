# 2026-10-11 — RTL8812CU VTX cannot transmit 40 MHz rungs

**Status:** fixed in devourer (branch `cu-ht40-tx`, commit "jaguar3: 8822C
40 MHz inject — DISDATAFB=1 and an IGI floor on the C die"); the full
record lives there in `docs/experiments/8822c-ht40-tx-stall.md`. mabur
itself needs no change — a drone with an 8822C needs `maburd` rebuilt
against that devourer (`tools/build-arm.sh`). The 8822E path is
byte-identical to before and has not been re-flown since.

Trigger: a CU VTX glitches on the 40 MHz rungs. On the bench (txdemo on
the CU, rxdemo on a GS card) the CU accepts 257 × 1 kB HT40 frames and
then stalls with nothing on air, on every channel; HT20 is perfect.

What it was, in two parts, both 40 MHz-only on the 8822C:

1. devourer's Jaguar3 inject descriptor had DISDATAFB=0; with a
   descriptor-fixed rate the MAC's fallback lookup hits a firmware
   per-MACID entry the injecting MACID never gets, and the first 40 MHz
   frame wedges the TX FIFO.
2. With that fixed, quiet channels still hang while busy ones work: a
   40 MHz TX needs the secondary 20 MHz idle, and below IGI 0x24 the
   8822C receiver never reads it idle on a silent channel; any received
   frame (or a higher IGI) clears it. That is exactly a two-way FPV link
   with RCF gaps: video stalls whenever the drone's receiver goes quiet
   for longer than the wedge onset (between 20 and 100 ms by the
   149-vs-jammer data). Fix: IGI floor 0x26 at 40/80 MHz on the C die,
   ~6 dB of idle-channel RX sensitivity there.

Ruled out on the way: firmware RA entries (H2C 0x40, every variant), the
firmware version (vendor v9.17 vs rtw88 v9.9), IQK, TX power, USB port,
interference, CCA disable, DFS. Mainline rtw88 as an HT40 AP on the same
card was the working reference (5.2 k MCS15/40 frames in 45 s).

Verified with no knobs: 144/52/149/36 at HT40 ≈ 20.5 k frames per 50 s,
0 failures, every frame received at MCS3/40; 144 HT20 unchanged.
