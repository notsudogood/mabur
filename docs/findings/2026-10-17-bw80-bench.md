# 80 MHz (VHT80) bench, 2026-10-17

Bench only, indoors: drone (SSC338Q + RTL8812EU, SoC at its stock 800 MHz)
and the RunCam WiFiLink GS (RK3566, two RTL8812EU) on the gs7 / d7 images
with `linkbench --bw 80`. Part 1 static at close range; Part 2 a 14-minute
range run with the drone two rooms away, channel 132. One pair, one day.

## Results

| What | Measured | Counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| MCS3/80 delivered fraction of nominal | 0.76 | One pair, one channel. |
| MCS4/80 delivered fraction | ≥ 0.67 | A floor, not a value: the run was CPU-limited at 117 Mb/s (FEC 0.15), so the radio's own ceiling is unmeasured. |
| Drone cpu0 at max throughput | ~94 % | linkbench only, no camera/encoder running. |
| Drone cpu0 at the 50 Mb/s video point (FEC 0.2) | ~60 % | linkbench only; maburd with the encoder is estimated at 80–85 %, not measured. |
| Reach, MCS4/80 vs MCS5/40 | MCS4/80 ~2–2.5 dB further | One range run; under the ±3-point delivery noise floor at single cells. |
| GS card tuned 80 hearing 20/40 frames | ~1–1.5 dB worse (up to ~3 on one card) | Why the GS cards sit at 40 and widen only for the 80 rungs (docs/bw80.md). |
| Narrow (20/40) frames on an 80-tuned card | Received | Not measured for loss at the edge. |

## What it decided

- The 80 MHz top replaces MCS5/40: rungs MCS3/80 (35 Mb/s cap) and MCS4/80
  (50 Mb/s cap), with light FEC (0.4/0.1 and 0.2/0.1) because the drone CPU,
  not the radio, is the limit at 50 Mb/s.
- `air_clock.efficiency_80 = [0.76 ×4, 0.67 ×4]` (`drone/src/config.h`):
  MCS0–3 take MCS3's measured 0.76, MCS4–7 take MCS4's floor.
- Channel set `[36, 132]`: the two 80 MHz blocks with no 8812EU spur
  (spur centres 58, 106, 122, 155, 171). 149 works but carries one.
