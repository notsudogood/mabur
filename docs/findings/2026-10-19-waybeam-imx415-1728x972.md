# 100 fps at 90 % of the sensor: waybeam's IMX415 driver and its 1728x972 mode

**Status:** spike, not shipped. The image still loads the stock OpenIPC
`sensor_imx415_mipi.ko`, and the shipped above-90-fps mode is its
1472x816@120 (`docs/airtime-model.md` §3, "Sensor mode and the shutter cap
follow `venc.fps`"). Builds on `docs/findings/2026-10-19-100fps.md`.

Bench, 2026-10-19, on `fps100` d8c3b11d plus two throwaway edits (below).
Question: can 100 fps keep the full field of view, and if not, how close
can it get?

## Field of view of the stock 1472x816 mode

Same scene, same build, snapshots (`GET /snapshot.jpg`) in mode 2
(1920x1080@60) and mode 3 (1472x816@100): two ceiling lights either side of
the main chandelier are 810 px apart in both, and the chandelier sits at
the same offset from the image centre (+190/−180 vs +181/−178 px). Mode 3
is the same 2x2-binned scale, a centred 2944x1632 window of the 3840x2160
array: 77 % × 76 % of the view, 58 % of the area (a 1.30x crop).

## Why full FOV stops at 90 fps

From waybeam_venc's device-mapped IMX415 study on the same SoC
(`documentation/STAR6E_IMX415_HEADROOM.md` §2 and §5.6, waybeam 0c880d8):
a frame is VMAX lines × HMAX. Full height binned needs VMAX ≥ 2250 (2160
rows + blanking); a wide binned line needs HMAX ≥ ~365 (4.92 µs) or the
readout is malformed (black frames with coloured lines at the correct fps).
74.25 MHz / (2250 × 365) = 90.4 fps. waybeam built full-FOV 1080p binned
modes at 100 and 120 fps with HMAX 328 / 275, saw exactly that garbage, and
removed them. Neither clock nor bit depth moves either floor.

At HMAX 365 a 100 fps frame allows VMAX 2034, which holds 972 binned lines
(2 × 972 + 90): waybeam's widest clean binned mode at 100 fps is 1728x972,
a centred 3456x1944 window, 90 % × 90 % of the view (81 % of the area, a
1.11x crop), 16:9.

## The driver swap

waybeam's `sensors/star6e/sensor_imx415_star6e.ko` (built from
`drivers/sensor_imx415_star6e.c` at its last change, fa0b40d) carries
`vermagic=4.9.84` like the drone's kernel and the same module parameters
(`chmap`, `mclk`, `lane_num`, ...). Copied over
`/lib/modules/4.9.84/sigmastar/sensor_imx415_mipi.ko` (the name
`load_sigmastar` insmods) and rebooted; it loads as
`sensor_imx415_star6e`. It enumerates five modes:

| idx | mode | stock driver's idx at that slot |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 3840x2160@33 | 3840x2160@30 |
| 1 | 2816x1584@60 (from a 2952x1656 crop) | 2560x1440@60 |
| 2 | 1920x1080@90, full FOV binned | same |
| 3 | 1728x972@100, binned wide crop | 1472x816@120 |
| 4 | 1728x816@120 | — |

so mabur's pinned indices (2 at ≤ 90 fps, 3 above, 0 for a 4K recording)
pick the matching modes with no mapping change. The driver also clamps the
exposure to the frame budget itself rather than stretching VMAX.

## 1728x972@100 on the bench

`venc.size = "1280x720"`, `fps = 100` (the loader's 1472:816 check
relaxed to 16:9 for this). `VPE scaling: 1728x972 -> 1280x720`, no precrop;
the snapshot is a clean image, visibly wider than mode 3 (lights 600 px
apart at 1280 wide = 810 px at the native 1728, mode 2's scale: 90 % of
the width confirmed). Exposure cap 9000 µs (the shipped high-fps cap).

Trim: `round 1 measured 100.010 Hz -> request 99979 mfps`, locked at
99.960 Hz in 1–2 rounds (`request 99958` / `99969 mfps`). Encoder
99.96 fps (1 s) / 99.93 (10 s); VPE `DropCnt` 11 at start, +1 in 3 min.

### The mode's maxFps is exactly 100

The first locked minute unlocked for 16 of 61 windows, `replaced` +10:
every phase-lock nudge above the mode's maximum was refused,

    [sensor] steer: MI_SNR_SetFps(100086 mfps) refused -1608835041, keeping 99894 mfps

(37 in the first minutes, requests 100018–100158). The steer keeps the last
accepted request on a refusal, which was a slower one, so the phase walks
off. The stock 1472x816 mode's maxFps is 120 and never refuses here. A
throwaway clamp of the steer request to `mode.maxFps × 1000` in
`star6e_runtime.c` removed every refusal.

### With the clamp

| window | locked | late | pdrop | replaced | skips | e2e p50/p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90 s | 86 / 92 | +0 | +0 | +1 | +0 | 24/24 ms |
| 180 s | 167 / 181 (one unlock, 14 windows) | +0 | +0 | +9 | +1 | 25–27 ms |

|lerr| mean 0.97 ms, max 3.92 ms over the 180 s. For comparison the stock
1472x816@100 run (same day, d8c3b11d) held 61/61 locked windows in its
minute with `replaced` +1, e2e 27–28 ms.

## On the Avatar Goggles X

The GS's HDMI moved from the monitor to a pair of Avatar Goggles X. Their
EDID (product name `AAABox`):

- two detailed timings, both at exactly 100.000 Hz: **1280x720@100**
  (123.75 MHz, the first DTD, so the preferred timing) and
  **1920x1080@100** (244.64 MHz, 2200 × 1112 total);
- everything else 50/60 Hz (24 Hz at 1080p);
- range limits 50–76 Hz V, 30–80 kHz H, 250 MHz: a custom 90 Hz timing is
  outside them, so on these goggles it is 100 Hz or 60.

`screen_mode = "1920x1080@100"` (the goggles' panel size):
`DrmPresenter: mode 1920x1080 @100.000 Hz`. The video plane scales
whatever the drone encodes to that size; the OSD is drawn at it.

With the clamp build, two encode sizes from the same 1728x972@100 mode,
spot readings after the lock (no soak):

| `venc.size` | trim | encoder | enc p50/p90 | fec p50/p90 | e2e p50/p90 | lock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1280x720 (VPE downscale) | locked, request 99948 mfps, 99.960 Hz | 100.02 fps | 4/5 ms | 2/5 ms | 27/27 ms | `lerr` −0.18 ms, offset +43 |
| 1728x972 (1:1, no scale) | locked, request 99958 mfps, 99.960 Hz | 99.96 fps | 6/8 ms | 4/8 ms | 32/32 ms | `lerr` −0.57 ms |

At 1728x972 the VENC device reads `MbRate 1054080`, **158 %** of its rated
macroblock rate (the 1280x720@100 channel reads `MbRate 576000`, 86 %;
the counter scales with the channel's macroblock count); it held 100 fps on the bench with the
480 MHz VENC overclock, with little headroom left for a busy scene. Against
the goggles' exact 100.000 Hz this mode works at its ceiling: VMAX 2034 is
100.008 Hz and 2035 is 99.959 Hz, so the lock has to alternate between the
two lines, and requests above 100000 mfps are refused (the clamp keeps it
at 100000).

## Open

- The once-per-~1.5 min unlock at 1728x972 was not explained. Candidates:
  the clamp leaves no request above the 100.008 Hz line (VMAX 2034 is this
  mode's floor), or this driver maps milli-fps to lines differently from the
  stock one (100000 mfps read 100.010 Hz here, 99.721 Hz on stock mode 3).
- Shipping it needs the module in the openipc-builder image, a real
  (tested) steer clamp to the mode's maxFps, the loader's high-fps aspect
  check changed to 16:9, and the reference pages moved off 1472x816.
- The other waybeam modes (4K@33, 2816x1584@60, 1728x816@120) were not run.
- No lock soak against the goggles (spot readings only), and the encode at
  1728x972 was not soaked for dropped frames under a busy scene.
- Not flown.
